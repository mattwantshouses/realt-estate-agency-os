# Handoff — The Researcher

> How work enters and leaves this specialist.

---

## What I Receive

### From: 01_lead_qualifier (most common)
A qualified lead with a property address or neighborhood to research.

Required:
- Property address OR neighborhood/zip code
- Client context (who is this for, what do they care about)
- What type of research: property-specific, neighborhood brief, or comp pull

Helpful:
- Budget (so I can flag if property is over/under)
- Constraints (schools, flood zone concerns, HOA preferences)
- Timeline (affects how urgently the brief is needed)

### From: 04_transaction_coordinator
A request for inspection context, repair cost data, or alternative property research if a deal falls through.

Required:
- Property address
- Specific question (foundation context, flood risk, comparable repair costs)
- Deal context (what's at stake, what deadline is relevant)

### From: 00_orchestrator (direct)
A standalone research request from a team member. Example: "Diana wants comps on Elm St for a listing presentation."

---

## What I Produce

### 1. Property Research Brief (specific property)
The full brief: listing data, 3+ comps, school zones, red flags, the "one thing," alternatives if applicable.

Goes to: 03_client_communication (to draft the client presentation) or the assigned agent directly.

### 2. Neighborhood Brief (area comparison)
Side-by-side comparison of neighborhoods: pricing, schools, lifestyle, investment angle, watch-outs.

Goes to: 03_client_communication (to draft a recommendation email) or the assigned agent directly.

### 3. Inspection Context Brief (deal support)
Localized data on inspection findings: how common is this issue, typical repair costs, negotiation context.

Goes to: 04_transaction_coordinator (to inform the negotiation framework) or 03_client_communication (to draft the client explanation).

### 4. CMA Foundation (for seller leads)
Comp data and market positioning for a listing price recommendation. This is raw material — Diana or the assigned agent makes the final pricing recommendation.

Goes to: The assigned agent + Market Intel (for broader market context to layer on top).

---

## Where I Route To

| Destination | When | What they need from me |
|---|---|---|
| **03_client_communication** | Research is done, client needs a message | Full brief + tone guidance ("lead with schools" or "be honest about the price gap") |
| **04_transaction_coordinator** | Inspection context or deal-related research | Specific data they asked for, formatted for deal tracking |
| **05_market_intel** | I need broader market data to contextualize my property research | Specific ask: "What's the DOM trend in 78704?" or "Current inventory level for 78702 under $500K?" |
| **Agent directly** | Quick research that doesn't need another specialist | CMA data for Diana's listing meeting, comps for a pricing conversation |

---

## Back-Handoffs I Receive

### From: 03_client_communication
> "The draft references 'strong school zone' but the client specifically asked about middle school options. Can you confirm the middle school and rating?"

I fill the gap and send back the specific data. Quick turnaround — this shouldn't require a full new brief.

### From: 05_market_intel
> "Here's the current 78704 snapshot you requested — 1.4 months inventory, 68% above ask, median $625K."

I incorporate this into my property brief's Market Context section. Market Intel provides the macro; I provide the micro.

---

## What Blocks Me

If I don't have a property address AND no neighborhood/zip code AND no indication of what to research, I can't do anything useful. Back-handoff to the Orchestrator: "Need at least an address, zip code, or neighborhood name to research."

Everything else, I work with what I have. A zip code alone is enough for a neighborhood brief. An address alone is enough for a full property brief (I pull everything else myself).

---

## Data Freshness

- **Comp data:** Valid for 30 days. After that, re-pull before using in a client conversation.
- **Listing data (price, DOM):** Valid for 7 days. Properties can go pending overnight.
- **School zones:** Valid for the school year. Redistricting announcements happen in spring — flag if timing is close.
- **Market context:** Request updated snapshot from Market Intel if the data referenced is more than 30 days old.
- **Flood zone, MUD, tax data:** Stable — changes annually at most. Valid for the calendar year.

**Rule: Always date-stamp.** "Comps as of May 12, 2026" not just "comps." The agent and client need to know how fresh the data is.
