# Transaction Routing Guide

> Read this before building any deal timeline or checklist. The type of transaction determines which checklist applies, what timeline to build, and which documents are required.
>
> Transaction Coordinator reads this on every new deal before doing anything else.

## Quick Reference: Which Contract Form?

| Situation | Contract |
|---|---|
| MLS purchase, buyer financing | FAR/BAR Residential Contract + Financing Rider |
| MLS purchase, buyer cash or all AS IS | FAR/BAR AS IS Residential Contract |
| RMD off-market cash purchase | FAR/BAR AS IS or custom PSA |
| RMD assignment/wholesale | FAR/BAR AS IS + Assignment Agreement |
| Listing agreement | Exclusive Right of Sale Listing Agreement (FAR form) |

## Florida-Specific Notes

- **No option period in Florida.** The inspection/due diligence period is the buyer's termination window. Once it expires, terminating risks the earnest money (unless another contingency applies).
- **Inspection period deadline:** 5:00 PM on the last day — not midnight. Never assume midnight.
- **Earnest money** is typically held by the title company (not the broker's trust account in all cases). Confirm with title at contract execution.
- **Documentary stamp taxes:** Seller pays $0.70 per $100 of purchase price on the deed. Buyer on a financed deal pays $0.35 per $100 on the mortgage note. Include in estimates.
- **CDD fees:** Common in planned communities (Nocatee, RiverTown, Durbin, Oakleaf). Annual charge, appears on property tax bill — not the same as HOA. Disclose to buyer upfront.
- **Closing Disclosure (CD):** Required T-3 (three business days before closing) for financed transactions. Cash transactions use a HUD-1 / settlement statement — no CD required.
- **Title work:** Conducted by a title company (or real estate attorney) in Florida. Heritage Title of NE Florida is Matt's current preferred title company — confirm on each deal.
