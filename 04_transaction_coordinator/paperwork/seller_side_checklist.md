# Seller Side — Transaction Checklist

> Used by Transaction Coordinator when Matt is representing the seller as a listing agent.
>
> Note: When RMD Home Buyers is purchasing from a motivated seller (investor mode), use `buyer_side_checklist.md` Track 3 — not this file. This file covers Matt acting as the seller's agent only.

---

## Track 1: Traditional MLS Listing

### Pre-Listing
- [ ] Exclusive Right of Sale Listing Agreement signed by all sellers
- [ ] Property address and legal description confirmed
- [ ] Seller's Property Disclosure Statement completed and signed
  - Review for: roof age and condition, HVAC age, plumbing, electrical, water intrusion history, pest/termite history, known defects
- [ ] Lead-Based Paint Disclosure signed (required if home built before 1978)
- [ ] HOA Disclosure completed and signed (if applicable): monthly fees, rules, pending assessments, reserve fund status
- [ ] CDD annual fee and outstanding balance disclosed (if applicable)
- [ ] Estimated Net Proceeds sheet reviewed with seller
  - Include: doc stamps on deed ($0.70 per $100 of sale price), title fees, commission, mortgage payoff
- [ ] Mortgage payoff balance confirmed (order formal payoff 2 weeks before closing)
- [ ] MLS input form completed (accurate specs, showing instructions, lockbox setup)
- [ ] Professional photos scheduled
- [ ] Active listing submitted to NEFMLS

### Under Contract (Offer Accepted)
- [ ] Offer reviewed with seller — key terms noted: price, earnest money amount, inspection period length, AS IS vs. standard, closing date, possession
- [ ] Counter-offer or acceptance executed
- [ ] Executed contract distributed to: seller, Matt, listing broker, title company
- [ ] Earnest money receipt from title company confirmed
- [ ] Deal timeline built — seller-side dates populated (inspection expiration, closing, possession)

### Inspection Period (Days 1–15, or per contract)
- [ ] Inspector access coordinated with seller
- [ ] Seller's inspection period obligations confirmed (maintaining utilities, access for inspector)
- [ ] Buyer's written inspection notice received (if submitted)
- [ ] Matt reviews notice with seller:
  - Options: accept credit request, counter-propose different credit/repair, decline (buyer decides to proceed or terminate)
- [ ] Seller's written response sent within agreed timeframe
- [ ] Resolution confirmed in writing before inspection period expires

### Post-Inspection / Pre-Closing
- [ ] Appraisal access coordinated (lender will schedule through listing agent or seller)
- [ ] Appraisal at value? If short: renegotiation or contract amendment needed — flag immediately
- [ ] Survey: access coordinated if buyer's lender requires
- [ ] Title commitment received — exceptions reviewed
  - Any title issues (liens, judgments, HOA violations, open permits) flagged to Matt immediately
- [ ] Payoff statement ordered from seller's lender (~14 days before closing)
- [ ] HOA resale package requested if applicable (some associations charge a fee; initiate early)
- [ ] Open permits resolved (if any exist on property — can delay or kill closing)

### Clear to Close / Closing Prep
- [ ] Buyer's lender CTC received (track from lender timeline)
- [ ] Closing Disclosure reviewed by seller (shows net proceeds)
- [ ] Possession date confirmed with seller (same day as closing vs. post-close)
- [ ] Final walkthrough date coordinated with buyer's agent
- [ ] Seller confirms utilities stay on through walkthrough and closing
- [ ] Seller confirms all personal property and debris removed per contract terms

### Closing Day
- [ ] Final walkthrough completed by buyer — any unresolved issues addressed
- [ ] Closing appointment at title company
- [ ] Seller signs warranty deed and all closing documents
- [ ] Payoff confirmed: seller's lender paid at closing
- [ ] Net proceeds confirmed and disbursed to seller
- [ ] Keys, garage openers, codes, HOA documents delivered to buyer
- [ ] Commission confirmed and disbursed
- [ ] Deal marked closed

## Track 2: Distress / Motivated Seller Listing

> Use when the seller is behind on payments, in pre-foreclosure, facing a short sale, or in a situation where traditional listing may not be viable.

⚠️ **Flag to Matt immediately on intake.** Distress situations have time sensitivity and may require lender involvement (short sale bank approval) or estate/probate attorney coordination.

### Additional Pre-Listing Steps (in addition to Track 1)
- [ ] Reason for distress documented (behind on payments, probate, divorce, relocation, other)
- [ ] Mortgage balance confirmed — is a short sale needed? (Sale price < payoff balance)
  - If short sale: bank (lender) must approve the sale before it can close. Expected timeline: 30–120 additional days.
- [ ] Other liens confirmed (HOA delinquency, IRS lien, judgments)
- [ ] Estate/probate status confirmed if applicable: is Matt working with a personal representative? Is the estate open in circuit court?
- [ ] Attorney referral offered to seller — Matt does not provide legal advice on estate or distress situations

### Short Sale Specific
- [ ] Hardship letter drafted (seller signs)
- [ ] Bank's short sale package submitted to lender's loss mitigation department
- [ ] BPO (Broker Price Opinion) expected from lender — track when scheduled
- [ ] Bank approval received
- [ ] Bank approval expiration date noted — closing must occur before approval expires

## Key Florida Seller Documents

| Document | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exclusive Right of Sale Listing Agreement | ✅ | Standard FAR form |
| Seller's Property Disclosure Statement | ✅ | Mandatory in FL for residential sales |
| Lead-Based Paint Disclosure | ✅ (pre-1978) | Federal requirement |
| HOA Disclosure | ✅ (if HOA exists) | Fees, rules, pending assessments |
| CDD Disclosure | ✅ (if CDD exists) | Annual fee amount and current balance |
| Estimated Net Proceeds | Best practice | Not legally required; essential for seller expectations |
| Executed Purchase Contract | ✅ (when offer accepted) | FAR/BAR or AS IS |
| Payoff Statement | ✅ | Order from seller's lender ~14 days before closing |
| Warranty Deed | ✅ | Prepared by title company; signed at closing |
| 1099-S | Tax reporting | Title company issues; flag if seller has basis concerns |

## Florida Documentary Stamp Taxes — Seller's Cost

**Seller pays doc stamps on the deed:**
- Rate: $0.70 per $100 of the purchase price
- Example: $400,000 sale → $2,800 in doc stamps

Include in the Estimated Net Proceeds sheet so the seller knows this cost before signing the listing agreement.
