# Buyer Side — Transaction Checklist

> Used by Transaction Coordinator for all deals where Matt is representing the buyer (agent mode) or where RMD Home Buyers is purchasing directly (investor mode).
>
> See `routing_guide.md` to confirm which track applies before starting.

---

## Track 1: Buyer Client — Financed (FAR/BAR + Financing Rider)

### Pre-Contract
- [ ] Buyer pre-approval letter on file (lender, max loan amount, expiration date)
- [ ] Proof of earnest money funds available

### At Contract Execution (Day 0)
- [ ] Executed FAR/BAR contract (all parties signed, Effective Date noted)
- [ ] Financing rider attached and executed
- [ ] Lead-Based Paint Disclosure signed (required if home built before 1978)
- [ ] HOA/Condo rider attached and signed (if applicable)
- [ ] Deal timeline built — all dates populated in TC rules

### Inspection Period (Days 1–15, or per contract)
- [ ] Inspection scheduled within 3 days of Effective Date
- [ ] Earnest money deposited with title company (by Day 3)
- [ ] EMD receipt from title company confirmed
- [ ] Seller's Property Disclosure received and reviewed
- [ ] Home inspection completed
- [ ] Inspection report reviewed by Matt
- [ ] Decision made: proceed as-is, request credit/repair, or terminate
  - If requesting credit or repair: written inspection notice sent to seller's agent before 5:00 PM on expiration date
  - Seller's response received and resolution confirmed (credit, repair, or proceed)
- [ ] Inspection period expires — buyer's decision confirmed in writing before 5:00 PM

### Post-Inspection / Pre-Closing
- [ ] Appraisal ordered by lender
- [ ] Appraisal received — at value? (If short: renegotiation or contract amendment needed — flag immediately)
- [ ] Survey ordered (if required by lender or title)
- [ ] Survey received and reviewed
- [ ] Title commitment received (Days 15–25)
- [ ] Title commitment reviewed for exceptions (flag anything unusual to Matt)
- [ ] CDD annual fee confirmed and disclosed to buyer (if applicable)
- [ ] Homeowner's insurance bound

### Clear to Close / Closing Prep
- [ ] Conditional approval from lender
- [ ] All loan conditions cleared
- [ ] Clear to Close (CTC) received from lender
- [ ] Final VOE completed (T-3 to T-1)
  - ⚠️ Client warned: no job changes, no new credit lines, no large purchases until after closing
- [ ] Closing Disclosure received by buyer — T-3 (three business days before closing)
- [ ] Buyer reviews CD — any discrepancies flagged to title and lender
- [ ] Closing date, time, and location confirmed with title company
- [ ] Wire instructions confirmed directly with title company (verify by phone — fraud risk)
- [ ] Final walkthrough scheduled (T-1 or day of)

### Closing Day
- [ ] Final walkthrough completed — any issues noted and addressed
- [ ] Buyer wires funds (or brings certified check)
- [ ] Documents signed at title company
- [ ] Deed recorded
- [ ] Keys delivered
- [ ] Commission confirmation received
- [ ] Deal marked closed in tracking system

## Track 2: Buyer Client — Cash (No Financing)

### At Contract Execution
- [ ] Executed FAR/BAR AS IS contract (all parties signed)
- [ ] Proof of funds letter submitted to listing agent
- [ ] Lead-Based Paint Disclosure (if pre-1978)
- [ ] HOA/Condo rider (if applicable)
- [ ] Deal timeline built — cash timeline (14–21 days)

### Inspection / Due Diligence (Days 1–10, or per contract)
- [ ] Earnest money deposited (Day 1–3)
- [ ] EMD receipt from title company
- [ ] Inspection scheduled and completed
- [ ] Seller's Property Disclosure received
- [ ] Decision: proceed, request credit/repair in writing, or terminate (before 5:00 PM on expiration date)

### Pre-Closing
- [ ] Title search ordered (initiate Day 1 on fast closes)
- [ ] Title commitment received
- [ ] Survey ordered (if desired)
- [ ] Closing date confirmed

### Closing
- [ ] Settlement statement (HUD-1) reviewed by buyer — T-1 minimum
- [ ] Wire instructions confirmed with title (verify by phone)
- [ ] Funds wired
- [ ] Documents signed
- [ ] Deed recorded

---

## Track 3: Investor Cash Purchase (RMD Home Buyers Buying Directly)

### At Contract Execution
- [ ] PSA or FAR/BAR AS IS executed
- [ ] Assignment clause confirmed in contract (if planning to assign)
- [ ] RMD proof of funds on file
- [ ] Earnest money deposited (Day 1–3)
- [ ] EMD receipt from title company

### Due Diligence (Days 1–7 or 1–10, per contract)
- [ ] Property walk-through completed
- [ ] Inspection / contractor scope of work (if full renovation deal)
- [ ] ARV confirmed against research brief
- [ ] Title search initiated Day 1 (fast close = no time to delay)
- [ ] Decision: proceed or terminate (before 5:00 PM on expiration date)

### If Assigning the Contract
- [ ] Assignment Agreement drafted and executed
- [ ] End buyer identified and qualified (proof of funds)
- [ ] Simultaneous close or assignment close logistics confirmed with title

### Closing
- [ ] Title commitment received
- [ ] Settlement statement (HUD-1) reviewed
- [ ] Funds confirmed (RMD's or end buyer's)
- [ ] Documents signed
- [ ] Deed recorded (warranty deed or quit claim — confirm with title)
- [ ] Deal marked closed
