# Examples — First Contact

## Example 1: Hot Buyer — Clear, Specific, Ready to Go

**Handoff card received from Orchestrator:**
> New Zillow lead. Sara M. mentions 1845 Westwood Dr 78704. Budget ~$550K. End of summer timeline. Two kids, schools matter.

**Qualification:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Buy |
| Budget | "around $550K" (her words — note the "around") |
| Timeline | End of summer (~3 months) — real deadline |
| Location | 78704, specifically mentions Westwood Dr |
| Constraints | Schools (2 kids), family-oriented neighborhood |
| Source | Zillow listing page |

**Temperature:** 🔥 Hot — Specific property, clear budget, real timeline, actionable constraints.

**Assignment:** → **Jess** (family + schools = her specialty in the roster)

**Handoff card produced → Property Research:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Property Research
Case:        Sara M. — qualified buyer, 78704
Agent:       Jess
Date:        2026-05-12
Confidence:  High

SUMMARY
Warm-Hot buyer. Specific property + clear timeline + stated budget.
Family with school-age kids driving the search. Assigned to Jess.
Needs property research + alternatives before first response.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Sara M.
- Property: 1845 Westwood Dr, 78704
- Intent: Buy
- Budget: ~$550K (firmness unknown — she said "around")
- Timeline: End of summer (~3 months)
- Temperature: Hot
- Constraints: Schools (2 kids), prefers 78704
- Source: Zillow

CONTEXT
No prior relationship. Zillow lead — no warm intro. She's done
enough research to name a specific address, which signals
seriousness. But "around $550K" in 78704 is tight — property may
be over budget.

⚡ Multiple offer alert: 78704 under $600K = expect competition.
Prep buyer for escalation clause discussion early.

GAPS
- Pre-approval status (critical — ask in first response)
- Budget firmness ($550K hard ceiling or flexible?)
- Currently renting or selling?
- Partner in decision?

NEXT ACTION
Research 1845 Westwood Dr: comps, condition, school zone. Pull
2-3 alternatives in 78704 under $550K with strong school zones.
```

## Example 2: Warm Lead — Real Intent, Missing Pieces

**Handoff card received from Orchestrator:**
> Instagram DM: "Hey! We've been thinking about buying our first home. No idea where to start, maybe East Austin? Is that even affordable anymore? 😅"

**Qualification:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Buy |
| Budget | Unknown |
| Timeline | Unknown — "been thinking about" suggests no urgency |
| Location | "maybe East Austin" — exploring, not committed |
| Constraints | First-time buyer (may need extra guidance on process) |
| Source | Instagram DM |

**Temperature:** 🟡 Warm — Real intent (they reached out), but no specifics yet. "No idea where to start" = early stage.

**Assignment:** → **Alex** (warm lead, first-time buyer energy, growth opportunity. Marcus available as backup if they get serious about East Austin specifically.)

**Handoff card produced → Client Communication:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Client Communication
Case:        Instagram DM — first-time buyer, East Austin curious
Agent:       Alex
Date:        2026-05-12
Confidence:  Medium

SUMMARY
Warm first-time buyer from Instagram. No budget, no timeline, no
specific property. Interested in East Austin but not committed.
Needs a nurture-first response — friendly, educational, no pressure.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Unknown name (Instagram DM, no last name given)
- Property: None — exploring
- Intent: Buy
- Budget: Unknown
- Timeline: Unknown — early stage
- Temperature: Warm
- Constraints: First-time buyer, curious about East Austin

CONTEXT
Instagram lead = lower commitment than Zillow (they didn't search
a specific property). "No idea where to start" means they need
education, not listings. The laughing emoji on "is that even
affordable anymore" suggests budget anxiety — don't lead with
prices, lead with "here's how the process works."

GAPS
- Name (ask naturally)
- Budget range (don't ask directly — feel out)
- Timeline (are they thinking 6 months? A year?)
- Pre-approval (way too early to ask — mention as a "when you're
  ready" next step)

NEXT ACTION
Draft a warm, low-pressure DM in Alex's voice. No listings. No
prices. Just: "Hey! Great question — East Austin is one of my
favorite areas. Happy to share what's realistic in different
pockets. Want to grab a quick coffee and I can walk you through
how the process works?" Get the conversation started.
```

**Why Client Communication instead of Property Research?** No property to research. No budget to anchor research to. This lead needs a conversation first, not data.

## Example 3: Seller Lead — Different Qualification Flow

**Handoff card received from Orchestrator:**
> Email from existing client: "Diana — we've been in our 78702 bungalow for 12 years. The kids are gone and we're thinking about downsizing. Not in a rush, but curious what our place might be worth. Can you take a look?"

**Qualification:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Sell (and likely Buy — downsizing means they need a new place) |
| Budget | N/A for sell side; buy side unknown |
| Timeline | "Not in a rush" — 6+ month timeline |
| Location | Current: 78702 (selling). Future: unknown (buying) |
| Constraints | Downsizing — smaller home. Empty nesters. |
| Source | Existing client — relationship already established |

**Temperature:** 🟡 Warm — Real intent, but no urgency. Existing relationship is an asset.

**Assignment:** → **Diana** (existing client, listing lead, and she'll want to handle the relationship personally)

**Handoff card produced → Market Intel:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Market Intel
Case:        Patterson — seller inquiry, 78702 bungalow
Agent:       Diana
Date:        2026-05-12
Confidence:  High

SUMMARY
Existing client wants to know what their 78702 bungalow is worth.
12 years of ownership, empty nesters downsizing. Not in a rush.
Diana handles the relationship. Need CMA-grade market data.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Patterson (existing client)
- Property: Their home — 78702 bungalow, 12 years owned
- Intent: Sell (and likely Buy to downsize)
- Budget: N/A for sell; buy TBD
- Timeline: 6+ months — exploratory
- Temperature: Warm
- Constraints: Downsizing, empty nesters

CONTEXT
This is a long-term client reaching back out. The tone is
conversational ("Diana —") not formal. They trust her. This is
a relationship play, not a hard sell. The CMA should be thorough
because it'll set expectations for the next 6 months.

Note: Once they're ready to list AND buy, this becomes a dual
engagement. Diana may need to coordinate both sides or bring in
a second agent for the buy side.

GAPS
- Property address (we know 78702 but not the specific address)
- Property details (sqft, bed/bath, condition, improvements)
- Mortgage payoff status
- Downsizing preferences (size, location, budget for new home)

NEXT ACTION
Market Intel: Pull 78702 bungalow market snapshot — recent sales,
price trends, DOM, what comparable homes are getting. Diana will
use this for a low-pressure CMA conversation.
```

**Why Market Intel instead of Property Research?** This is a seller lead. They don't need property research on someone else's home — they need market data on their own neighborhood. Market Intel produces the CMA foundation; Property Research would only get involved when the Pattersons start looking at properties to buy.
