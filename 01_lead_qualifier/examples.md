## Example 1: Hot Buyer — Referral, Pre-Approved, Hard Timeline

**Handoff from Orchestrator:**
> Referral from past client Brian Clark. Sister-in-law Rachel Torres, pre-approved up to $425K, moving from Atlanta for a job, needs to be in Jacksonville by mid-August. Contact: 904-555-0192.

**Qualification:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Buy |
| Budget | Pre-approved up to $425K |
| Timeline | Mid-August — hard deadline for job relocation |
| Location | Jacksonville — new to area, no neighborhood preference yet |
| Constraints | Relocation, job-driven timeline, may need school zone info |
| Source | Referral — Brian Clark (past client, closed 2025) |

**Temperature:** 🔥 Hot — Pre-approved, hard deadline, referral from past client.

**Lead type:** Buyer client — Agent mode.

**Handoff card produced → Client Communication:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Client Communication
Case:        Rachel Torres — referral from Brian Clark
Mode:        Agent
Date:        2026-05-26
Confidence:  High

SUMMARY
Pre-approved buyer, hard mid-August close deadline, referral
from Brian Clark (past client Matt knows personally). New to
Jacksonville — needs neighborhood education before property
tour.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Rachel Torres, 904-555-0192
- Intent: Buy
- Budget: Pre-approved up to $425K
- Timeline: Mid-August (hard — job relocation)
- Location: Jacksonville metro — no preference yet
- Temperature: Hot
- Source: Referral — Brian Clark

CONTEXT
Relocation from Atlanta. Pre-approved = financially ready.
Mid-August = hard deadline. With FL closing times (30–45 days
financed), needs to be under contract by early July at the
latest. Matt should acknowledge Brian in first message.

⚡ Timeline alert: ~11 weeks to close. Needs to be under
contract by early July.

GAPS
- Kids? (school zone priority?)
- Single family or condo?
- Partner/co-buyer?
- Job location (affects neighborhood recommendation)

NEXT ACTION
Draft Matt's first outreach to Rachel — warm intro, acknowledge
Brian, offer to walk her through Jacksonville neighborhoods.
```

**Note:** Client Communication goes first — Matt needs to make contact and understand her needs before Property Research can pull anything useful.

## Example 2: Motivated Seller — Cold Outreach Response

**Handoff from Orchestrator:**
> Text response to RMD direct mail. Phone: 904-555-0847. Message: "Yeah I might be interested. We've had the house about 12 years. Needs some work. What do you pay?"

**Qualification:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Sell — motivated (investor mode) |
| Budget | Unknown — price-curious ("what do you pay?") |
| Timeline | Unknown — "might be interested" suggests open, not urgent |
| Location | Unknown — in RMD's 32210 outreach zone (absentee owner list) |
| Constraints | "Needs some work" = deferred maintenance, likely as-is seller |
| Source | RMD direct mail — absentee owner campaign |

**Temperature:** 🟡 Warm — They responded to outreach. That's a signal. But "might be interested" isn't commitment.

**Lead type:** Motivated seller — Investor mode.

**Handoff card produced → Client Communication:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Client Communication
Case:        Unknown — RMD outreach response, 904-555-0847
Mode:        Investor
Date:        2026-05-26
Confidence:  High

SUMMARY
Inbound text from absentee owner outreach. Open to selling,
12-year ownership, property needs work. Price-curious but not
committed. Investor mode — Matt responds as RMD Home Buyers.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Unknown name (text only)
- Property: Unknown — somewhere in 32210 (outreach zone)
- Intent: Sell — motivated/cash interest
- Budget: Unknown
- Timeline: Unknown — exploratory
- Temperature: Warm
- Constraints: Deferred maintenance, likely as-is
- Source: RMD direct mail, absentee owner list

CONTEXT
Long-term absentee owner, deferred maintenance, responds to
outreach with a price question. Not urgent, but not dismissing
either. Matt should build rapport first — get the address and
hear the story before talking numbers.

GAPS
- Name
- Property address
- Reason considering selling
- Mortgage/lien status
- Extent of needed work

NEXT ACTION
Draft Matt's response as RMD — warm, genuine, no pressure.
Goal: get the address and get them talking. Not a pitch.
```

## Example 3: Ambiguous — Listing or Cash Offer?

**Handoff from Orchestrator:**
> Email from website contact form. "Hi, I got your info from a neighbor. We're thinking about selling our house in the Julington Creek area. Would love to know what you think about the market and what we could get for it."

**Qualification attempt:**

| Field | Captured |
|---|---|
| Intent | Sell — MODE UNCLEAR (listing or cash offer?) |
| Budget | N/A for sell side |
| Timeline | Unknown — "thinking about" |
| Location | Julington Creek area (St Johns County, ~32259) |
| Constraints | None stated |
| Source | Neighbor referral |

**Temperature:** 🟡 Warm — Referral from a neighbor. Real intent to sell. No urgency.

**Lead type:** UNCLEAR — Could be seller wanting MLS listing (agent mode) OR seller interested in a cash offer (investor mode). "What you think about the market" leans toward listing curiosity, but can't confirm without asking.

**Handoff card produced → Client Communication:**

```
From:        Lead Qualifier
To:          Client Communication
Case:        Unknown — Julington Creek seller, website form
Mode:        Unknown — needs clarification
Date:        2026-05-26
Confidence:  Medium

SUMMARY
Neighbor referral. Seller in Julington Creek, ~32259. Asking
about market value. Mode unclear — could be listing inquiry
(agent) or cash offer interest (investor). Matt needs a first
conversation before routing further.

KEY DETAILS
- Client: Unknown (website form, no name given)
- Property: Unknown — Julington Creek area, ~32259
- Intent: Sell — mode unknown
- Timeline: "Thinking about" — exploratory
- Temperature: Warm
- Source: Neighbor referral

CONTEXT
32259 (RiverTown/Durbin/Julington Creek) is a fast-moving St
Johns County market. Traditional listing here could move
quickly at strong prices. If they want a cash offer, investor
mode applies instead.

GAPS
- Name and contact info
- Property address
- Whether they want to list on market or sell directly
- Timeline and reason for selling

NEXT ACTION
Draft Matt's first response — warm, acknowledge the neighbor
referral, ask a natural question to clarify intent. Don't
pitch either mode until Matt understands what they want.
```

**Why Client Communication instead of Market Intel?** No address, and no way to know if they want a CMA (listing) or a cash offer conversation (investor). Matt needs a first conversation before any research is useful.
