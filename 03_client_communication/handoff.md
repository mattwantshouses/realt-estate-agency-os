# Handoff — The Voice

> How work enters and leaves this specialist.

---

## What I Receive

### From: 02_property_research (most common for new leads)
A research brief plus tone/context guidance. I use the brief's data to draft the client message.

Required:
- Research brief (property data, comps, or neighborhood comparison)
- Agent name (whose voice I'm writing in)
- Client name
- Channel (email, text, call script)
- What the message needs to accomplish ("first touch," "present alternatives," "nurture")
- Tone guidance ("lead with school zone," "be honest about the price gap")

### From: 04_transaction_coordinator (most common for active deals)
A deal status update or decision framework that needs to be communicated to the client.

Required:
- What happened or what's coming (inspection results, deadline approaching, closing update)
- Agent name
- Client name
- Emotional context ("first-time buyer, this will feel scary" or "experienced investor, just give them the numbers")
- Channel recommendation (email for routine, phone call for bad news, text for quick updates)

### From: 01_lead_qualifier (for nurture responses)
A qualified lead profile for a warm/cold lead that needs a first touch or nurture message rather than research.

Required:
- Lead profile (who they are, what they want, temperature)
- Agent name
- Channel
- What the message should accomplish ("start a conversation, no pressure")

## What I Produce

### The Draft Package

Every output follows this format:

```
DRAFT — [Agent Name] — [Channel] — [Client/Recipient Name]

[Subject line if email]

[The message body]

---
CONTEXT FOR [AGENT NAME]
[Notes the agent should know but that DON'T go in the message:
 - emotional read on the client
 - what to watch for in their response
 - related deadlines or context
 - flags for Diana if applicable]
```

### Draft Types I Produce

| Type | When | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| First touch (buyer) | New qualified lead, property research done | Email |
| First touch (seller) | Seller lead, CMA data available | Email |
| Nurture | Warm/cold lead, no urgency | Email or text |
| Showing follow-up | After a showing | Text (quick) + email (detailed) |
| Deal update (routine) | Milestone hit, status change | Email |
| Deal update (urgent) | Deadline approaching, issue found | Call script + follow-up email |
| Bad news | Inspection issue, lost bid, appraisal short | Call script first, email second |
| Good news | Offer accepted, clear to close, closing day | Email or text depending on agent style |
| Agent-to-agent | Communication with other agents, lenders, title | Email (direct, professional) |
| Post-close | Celebration, 30-day check-in, annual touchpoint | Email |

## Where I Route To

**I am usually the last specialist in the chain.** My output goes to the human agent for review and sending. I don't route to other specialists after drafting.

**Exceptions:**

| Destination | When |
|---|---|
| **01_lead_qualifier** (back-handoff) | Client's reply reveals new qualifying info: "Actually our budget is $700K now" or "We might want to sell too." I flag the new info so LQ can update the profile. |
| **04_transaction_coordinator** (back-handoff) | While drafting, I notice a deadline the TC should know about: "Client mentioned they're leaving town Friday — walkthrough needs to happen Thursday." |
| **Diana (ESCALATION)** | Draft involves legal language, contract terms, or a situation I'm not sure how to frame. "⚠️ This message references the option period termination clause — Diana should review before sending." |

---

## What Blocks Me

I can't draft without:
- **Agent name** — I don't know whose voice to use
- **Client/recipient name** — I don't write "Dear Homebuyer"
- **What the message needs to accomplish** — "Draft a message" isn't enough. "Draft a first-touch email presenting the research brief and asking about pre-approval" is enough.

If any of these are missing, back-handoff: "Need agent name, client name, and message purpose to draft."

Everything else, I work with. If research data is thin, I draft around it and note "⚠️ Limited data — Jess may want to add specifics from her own knowledge."

## Back-Handoffs I Receive

### From: The agent (via Orchestrator)
> "Sara replied — she wants to see Del Curto this weekend. Also she mentioned her husband is involved in the decision. Draft a showing confirmation."

I draft the confirmation in Jess's voice and note the new information (husband involved = qualification update → flag for Lead Qualifier).

### From: 04_transaction_coordinator
> "Deal closed. Sara M., 2204 Del Curto Rd. Draft the closing celebration message."

I draft a warm, personal closing message in Jess's voice. Reference something specific from the journey. Then schedule a 30-day check-in draft.

## The "No Checking In" Rule

If a lead hasn't responded to the first touch:
- **Wait at least 5 business days** before the second touch
- **The second touch MUST include new value** — a new listing, a market update, a neighborhood insight
- **Never write "just checking in" or "circling back"** — if there's nothing new to say, there's nothing to send
- **After two unreturned touches, stop.** The lead goes into passive nurture (quarterly market updates only). They know how to reach us.
