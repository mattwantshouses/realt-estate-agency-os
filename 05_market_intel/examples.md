## Example 1: Zip Code Snapshot — Supporting Property Research

**Handoff card received from Property Research:**
> Need 78704 market context for Sara M.'s property research. What's the current state of this zip code?

**Market intel produced:**

```
MARKET SNAPSHOT — 78704
As of: May 2026
Source: Austin Board of REALTORS / MLS

Median price:           $625,000 (↑ 8% YoY)
Active inventory:       47 listings
Months of inventory:    1.4 months
Avg days on market:     18 days
% selling above ask:    68%
Median price/sqft:      $415
New listings (30 days): 23

MARKET TYPE: Seller's Market

INTERPRETATION
78704 remains one of Austin's most competitive residential
zip codes. At 1.4 months of inventory, demand significantly
outpaces supply. 68% of homes are selling above asking price,
and the average listing lasts only 18 days.

For buyers: anything under $600K will likely see 3-5 offers
within the first week. Pre-approved buyers with escalation
clauses are winning over higher offers without them. Speed
and preparation matter more than raw bid price.

For sellers: well-priced listings in good condition are
clearing in under two weeks. Overpriced listings (note: DOM
above 30 = likely overpriced for condition) are stalling
because buyers have learned to be selective even in a hot
market.

RATE CONTEXT
30-yr fixed: 6.9% this week
At $535K purchase (20% down): $2,826/mo P&I
At $575K purchase (20% down): $3,037/mo P&I
The $40K price difference = $211/mo in payments.
```

**Handoff card produced → Property Research:**

```

Agent:       Jess
Date:        2026-05-12
Confidence:  High

SUMMARY
78704 snapshot attached. Seller's market. Sara's $550K budget
puts her at the lower end of the competitive range. Multiple
offers likely on any property she targets.

KEY DETAILS
[Full snapshot above]

CONTEXT
This data is fresh (May 2026 MLS pull). The multiple-offer
dynamic is the most important thing for Property Research to
include in the brief — it changes how Jess should prep Sara
for the buying process.

GAPS
None — this is a data delivery.

NEXT ACTION
Property Research: incorporate this context into Sara's
property brief. Flag the competitive reality in your
recommendation section.
```

## Example 2: Rate Impact Analysis — Active Deal Alert

**Self-triggered: weekly rate monitoring flagged a change.**

```
RATE CHANGE ALERT
As of: 2026-06-10
Source: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey

CHANGE
30-yr fixed moved from 6.8% → 7.1% this week (+0.30%)

IMPACT ON ACTIVE DEALS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Sara M. — 2204 Del Curto Rd ($535K, 20% down)
Previous rate: ~6.8% → $2,793/mo P&I
Current rate:  ~7.1% → $2,880/mo P&I
Difference:    +$87/mo
Risk: LOW — Sara has rate lock through July 15. Verify
lock is still valid with First National.

[Any other active buyers would be listed here]

IMPACT ON PIPELINE
Warm/cold leads without pre-approval should be re-assessed.
A buyer who qualified at $550K at 6.8% may only qualify at
~$530K at 7.1%. Lead Qualifier should flag this when
rating temperature for new leads.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE TEAM
Not a major shift, but enough to push borderline buyers
down a bracket. Agents should mention rate locks earlier
in the process — "locking in while rates are still in the
6s" is an honest and effective message right now.
```

**Routing:** Sent to Orchestrator for distribution. TC checks Sara's rate lock. Lead Qualifier notes the new context for incoming leads.

## Example 3: CMA Foundation — Seller Lead Support

**Handoff card received from Lead Qualifier:**
> Patterson family, existing client, wants to know what their 78702 bungalow is worth. 12 years owned, empty nesters. Diana handles.

**CMA foundation produced:**

```
CMA FOUNDATION — 78702 BUNGALOW
Prepared for: Diana Coleman
Client: Patterson family
As of: May 2026

78702 MARKET CONTEXT
Median price:           $520,000 (↑ 12% YoY)
Months of inventory:    1.1 months
Avg DOM:                14 days
% above ask:            72%
Price/sqft:             $425

BUNGALOW SEGMENT (78702, 2-3 bed, 900-1,400 sqft)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Recent sales (last 90 days):

1. 2108 E. 2nd St — $485,000 (32 days ago)
   2/1, 980 sqft. Original condition. Cash buyer.
   $495/sqft. Low for the area — condition drove it down.

2. 1804 E. Cesar Chavez — $545,000 (18 days ago)
   3/1, 1,180 sqft. Updated kitchen + bathroom.
   $462/sqft. Multiple offers, sold 5% above ask.

3. 907 Willow St — $510,000 (41 days ago)
   2/2, 1,100 sqft. Partial renovation (kitchen done,
   baths original). $464/sqft. Sold at ask.

4. 1612 Holly St — $575,000 (25 days ago)
   3/2, 1,320 sqft. Full renovation. New systems.
   $436/sqft. Sold in 8 days, 3% above ask.

PRICING ANALYSIS
The range for bungalows in 78702 is $485K-$575K depending
heavily on condition and updates. The spread between
"original" and "fully renovated" is roughly $80-90K.

Without knowing the Pattersons' specific property details
(sqft, bed/bath, condition, updates), I'd estimate:
- Original/minimal updates: $480-510K
- Moderate updates (kitchen or bath): $510-545K
- Fully updated: $545-580K

12-YEAR OWNERSHIP CONTEXT
If the Pattersons bought in 2014, they likely paid $180-250K
for a 78702 bungalow. At current values, they're looking at
roughly 2-3x appreciation. This is helpful context for
Diana's conversation — they're in a strong equity position
regardless of condition.

MARKET TIMING NOTE
Spring (now) is peak listing season in 78702. If they're
not in a rush, listing in the next 60 days captures the
best buyer pool. Fall is second-best. Winter is slowest.

GAPS
- Need the actual property address for Property Research
  to pull specific data (tax assessed value, lot size,
  any permits/improvements on record)
- Need condition assessment (Diana's eye or photos)

NEXT ACTION
Diana: use this as conversation starter. Get the address
and a sense of condition, then Property Research can
produce a full property-specific CMA.
```

**Why this goes to Diana, not Property Research first:** The Pattersons are existing clients reaching out to their person (Diana). She needs the market context to have the initial conversation. Once she has the address and condition details, THEN Property Research does the full CMA.
